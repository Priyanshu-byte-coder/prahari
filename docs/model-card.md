# Model card — Prahari ANPR (lane I)

Written for the people who will be asked to trust a number that came out of this system: a
judge, a duty officer, and whoever inherits this repo. Every measured figure in it comes from
`docs/accuracy-report.md`, which is generated by
`python scripts/accuracy_report.py --golden fixtures/golden/`. Nothing here is typed from
memory, and where a measurement does not exist yet this card says so rather than estimating.

## What it is

A pipeline, not a model: five stages, four of which are pretrained components chosen for
licence and availability, and one of which — the vote — is ours and is where the accuracy
claims actually come from.

| Stage | Component | Licence | Weights |
|---|---|---|---|
| vehicle detection | YOLOv8s (COCO) | AGPL-3.0 | `models/yolov8s.pt` |
| tracking | ByteTrack (ultralytics) | AGPL-3.0 | none |
| plate localisation | classical blackhat/Sobel proposal | ours | none |
| plate reading | EasyOCR (CRAFT+CRNN) · PaddleOCR (DB+SVTR) | Apache-2.0 | vendor defaults |
| fusion | grammar + per-character weighted vote | ours | none |
| re-identification | torchvision ResNet-18 trunk, 512-d | BSD-3-Clause | ImageNet |

Vehicle classes emitted: `two_wheeler car bus truck`. **Not** emitted, because COCO has no such
class and geometry is not evidence: `three_wheeler lcv tractor`. Fine-tuning (I11) is what adds
them; until then the field is honest about what it does not know.

## Intended use

Investigative retrieval: given a registration number, produce that vehicle's sightings across a
camera grid, with timestamps and crops, for a human to act on.

## Out of scope, explicitly

- **Automated enforcement.** No output of this pipeline should trigger a penalty without a human
  looking at the crop.
- **Identity.** Plate identity is the only identity here. The re-id vector corroborates a route
  hop and can never confirm one; they never share a field ([C1]).
- **Faces.** Not in this pipeline at all. It would need a separate legal authorisation.
- **Any camera whose plates land under ~100 px wide.** That is a siting fault, and no model
  fixes it. See "Known limits".

## Data

- **Golden set** (`fixtures/golden/`): hand-labelled crops and frames from grid footage, never
  trained on, split by camera and by day — never by frame, because frames from one pass are
  near-duplicates and a frame-level split reports memory as accuracy.
- **Synthetic set**: rendered plates on a real vehicle photograph (`services/worker/synth.py`).
  Exact ground truth, available today, and an **upper bound** — rendered glyphs, no real
  perspective, generator-controlled blur. Every row is tagged `synthetic` and the report keeps
  the two blocks apart. Deck numbers come from the hand-labelled split.
- Nothing is trained on the golden set. No training has happened at all yet; the detectors are
  pretrained (that decision, and its date, are in `knowledge_base.md`).

## Metrics — see `docs/accuracy-report.md` for the current run

The report gives, per reader and for the fused vote: exact-match rate, character error rate,
precision per band, and the refusal rate. Three of those matter more than the headline:

- **Precision at CONFIRMED must be effectively 1.0.** The band exists to be actionable. The
  report fails its own verdict line if a single CONFIRMED read is wrong.
- **The refusal rate is quoted next to exact match, always.** Roughly 13% of real passes cannot
  be read by any reader — dirt, angle, a two-wheeler's rear plate at night, a truck's tailgate
  down. Reporting exact match without the refusal rate turns a 70% system into a 95% claim.
- **CER, not just exact match.** It separates "one confusable digit" from "a different vehicle".

## How the vote decides — the part that is ours

1. `normalise` — upper case, strip separators and the HSRP `IND` band ([C7]).
2. `grammar_fix` — force each character into the class its slot demands, per the state/RTO/
   series/number grammar and the BH series. Confusion classes are resolved by position before
   anything is counted.
3. Vote per character position across up to 8 of the sharpest crops × every reader, weighted by
   reader confidence.
4. Emit `plate_text` only when **every** position reaches a 2/3 weighted majority and at least
   two independent reads agree. Otherwise `plate_text` is null and the crop is kept.
5. `CONFIRMED` additionally requires a grammar-valid string and three reads.

Sharpness (variance of the Laplacian) decides which crops get read at all, so the OCR budget is
spent on the frames that can change the answer.

## Known limits

- **Plate width under ~100 px** — recall falls off a cliff. This is a camera-siting parameter,
  and it belongs in the deployment guidance, not in a model tweak.
- **Night and headlight glare** — the plate face saturates; the classical proposal survives it
  better than a naive threshold, the readers do not.
- **Two-wheelers** — rear plate only, often bent, often obscured by a pillion. Expect the
  refusal rate on `two_wheeler` to be several times the fleet average.
- **Non-standard plates** — fancy fonts, state-specific layouts, temporary registrations. The
  grammar rejects them, so they refuse rather than misread. That is the correct failure.
- **The classical plate proposal** is the weakest link and the first thing I11 replaces with a
  trained detector; the swap is a path in `models/README.md`, not a code change.
- **One reader on a laptop.** The accuracy target assumes at least two readers voting. The
  pipeline logs a warning and widens the band when only one loads.

## Fairness and misuse

A plate is a property identifier, not a person. The system stores no demographic attribute and
derives none. Every read is banded, every export writes an audit row with a user and a reason
([C10]), and the route API flags physically implausible hops instead of drawing them silently.
The one design decision that matters most for misuse is the refusal: below a 2/3 majority the
system reports nothing, because a wrong registration handed to an officer is not a smaller
error than no registration — it is a different vehicle's owner at the door.
