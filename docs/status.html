Top risks

watch these
  1. Uploading on the last evening. The portal is slow when everyone does it — hence the 6 Sep 14:00 target.
  2. A video that looks like a mock-up. Explicitly rejected. Record the real thing even if it's uglier, with /metrics on screen.
  3. A deck number nobody can reproduce. Every figure traces to a command, or it comes off the slide.
  4. Credentials that don't work from outside the network. Test the hosted URL and all three logins from mobile data.
  5. Route coordinates still LOW-confidence (geocoded, not surveyed). Fine for the demo — say so out loud rather than let a judge find it.
