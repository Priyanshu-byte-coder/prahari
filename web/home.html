How it works

Four stages between a passing car and an answer.

Watch

Every camera decoded at once, with a motion gate so still frames never reach the GPU. A stalled feed is caught by a watchdog, not by a person noticing.

30 cameras, 5 fps each

Read

Vehicles are tracked across the pass, the sharpest crop is chosen, and two independent readers vote on the plate through the Indian plate grammar.

Two readers, one vote

Match

The read is matched against the watchlist by exact plate first, then by a confusion aware distance. A near miss raises a lower band, never a confirmation.

Exact, then near, then nothing

Trace

Sightings of one vehicle across cameras become an ordered route with implied speeds, so a hop that would need 300 km/h is flagged rather than drawn.

Ordered, speed checked

Against the brief

What the challenge asks for, and where it runs.

The problem statement names five solution models and asks for one, or a hybrid. Prahari is a hybrid of Models 1, 3 and 4: a camera registry on GIS, a federation layer over heterogeneous transports, and AI analytics on top. Every row below links to the screen where you can check it.

Registry and GIS

Every camera in the catalogue carries its district, transport, install type and a coordinate with an honest confidence grade. Cameras that cannot be placed are listed as unplaced rather than dropped on a district centroid.

Model 1 · foundation layer

Federation over vendors

One driver interface across MediaMTX, RTSP, ONVIF and a VMS stub. A camera moves between transports by re-probing, not by a code change, which is what makes 80,000 cameras across 26 departments tractable.

Model 3 · middleware

Analytics and correlation

ANPR against the watchlist, alerts on a match, and cross-camera vehicle history reconstructed as an ordered route with the impossible legs called out.

Model 4 · AI platform

Trust and refusal

A wrong plate is worse than no plate.

Two readers vote on every crop. Below two thirds agreement Prahari writes no plate at all, and the console shows the gap instead of filling it. Every number on screen carries the band it was read at.

CONFIRMED GJ01AB1234 Both readers agree after grammar repair. Safe to act on.
PROBABLE GJ01AB1Z34 One character from a watchlist entry, inside a known confusion class.
WEAK GJ01A?1234 Read, but under the vote floor. Shown as a gap, never guessed shut.
NONE no plate The pass is still recorded, with vehicle class and colour only.
